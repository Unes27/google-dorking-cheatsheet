Avoid stale lists

Retired or unreliable commands

Many online dork lists mix old features with current search syntax. These are not dependable commands in Google Search:

  • cache: — removed; no longer works in Google Search.
  • related: — removed from Google's supported operator documentation.
  • info: and link: — widely circulated, but unreliable or unsupported.
  • Leading + and ~ — legacy advice; do not rely on them for inclusion or synonym behavior.

Google does not publish a complete catalog of every query pattern people call a “dork.” Use the labels above, test a small query, and verify important findings in the live site or Search Console.
